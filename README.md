## 🌟 Feature Highlights (v3.7)

### 1. 🪟 Interactive Drag-to-Resize Picture-in-Picture (PiP)
- Press **`P`** or click the PiP button in the player bar to pop the video out into a floating mini-player pinned across all desktop Spaces.
- **Drag to Resize**: Grab the dedicated bottom-right grabber handle or any window edge to effortlessly expand or shrink the PiP window.
- Resizing automatically maintains a crisp, cinematic 16:9 aspect ratio from 260px up to 1280px.

### 2. 🎬 Mobile Remote Video Quality Switcher
- Choose your preferred resolution directly from the phone remote: **Auto (Adaptive)**, **1080p FHD**, **720p HD**, **480p SD**, or **360p Low**.
- Real-time quality badge updates instantly on both phone and Mac screens.

### 3. ⭐ Bidirectional Mac Favorites Synchronization
- The mobile remote now reads and displays all of your Mac's favorited channels under the **"⭐ Favorites"** category ribbon.
- Tapping the star icon on either phone or Mac syncs instantly across both devices and saves to persistent storage.

### 4. ⏮⏭ Dedicated Channel Rockers & Navigation
- Prominent **`CH +`** and **`CH -`** stepper rockers in the remote tab, plus quick skip buttons in the sticky Now Playing bar for effortless channel surfing.

### 5. 📱 Wireless Phone as TV Remote Control & Mobile Guide
- Click **"Phone Remote"** or press **`O`** on your Mac to show a QR code.
- Scan with your phone's camera on the same Wi-Fi:
  - Dual-view UI: Switch seamlessly between **Channels Guide (Home)** and **D-Pad Controller**.
  - Live channel search across 2,989 channels and instant category filtering.
  - In-app software update trigger: Check and stage macOS updates directly from your smartphone.

### 6. 📊 Broadcast Diagnostics & Telemetry HUD ("Stats for Nerds")
- Press **`I`** or click the Stats button to toggle a live broadcast telemetry HUD.
- Shows real-time video resolution, framerate, current bitrate (kbps), buffer health (seconds), dropped frame counter, active CDN host, and audio format.

### 7. 🎚️ Pro Audio Equalizer & Sound Profiles
- 🎧 **Normal (Flat):** Authentic original broadcast sound.
- 🏟️ **Stadium Atmosphere (Sports):** Boosts crowd ambient rumble and brings commentary speech forward for cricket and football.
- 🎙️ **Dialogue Clarity (News):** Enhances vocal frequencies while trimming room boom and bass muddiness.
- 🌙 **Night Mode:** Smart dynamic range compression so loud commercials or cheers don't wake people up.
- 🎵 **Music Mode:** Deep bass resonance and sparkling treble brilliance.

### 8. ⏱️ Bedtime Sleep Timer with Gentle Audio Fade-Out
- Set **15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, 90m, or 120m** timer.
- During the final 2 minutes, audio volume gently ramps down to zero before stopping playback, allowing you to fall asleep comfortably.

### 9. ⚡ One-Click Native In-App Auto-Updater
- Detects new GitHub releases in real time.
- Download, verify, install, and relaunch directly within the app without ever touching a browser.

### 10. 📺 Massive Catalog of 2,989+ HD Live Channels
- Extensive selection of Bangladeshi, Indian, USA, UK, and international broadcasts.
- **Includes Discovery Bangla, Discovery Kids Bangla, Discovery Science HD, Discovery Turbo HD** with multi-server failover.
- Categories include: **Bangladesh, Sports, News, Entertainment, Movies, Music, Kids, Documentary, and Religious**.
- Native HLS/DASH streaming with automatic quality switching up to **1080p HD & 4K UHD**.

### 11. 🔥 Real-Time Live Sports Telemetry Engine
- **100% genuine real-time data**: Live feeds from FanCode and ESPN API scorepanels.
- Dynamic match status indicators, live scores, overs/wickets/sets/match clocks.
- One-click tuning directly into active HD live match broadcasts for **Cricket, Football, Tennis, and MotoGP**.

### 12. 🔲 Multi-View Studio (Dual & Quad Stream)
- Watch **2 or 4 live streams simultaneously** on the same screen.
- Switch between **Dual (2-Up)** and **Quad (4-Up)** modes with a single click.
- Click any video quadrant to immediately route active audio focus to that channel.

### 13. 🕒 Recently Watched Carousel ("Jump Back In")
- Automatically tracks your last 15 tuned channels in a quick horizontal strip.
- Resume any stream instantly without browsing categories or searching.

### 14. 📌 Native macOS "Always On Top" Floating Mode
- Keeps Live TV floating above all other desktop applications (browsers, IDEs, Slack).
- Dedicated pin button (**📌**) or keyboard shortcut (**`T`** or `Cmd+T`).

### 15. 🔊 200% Web Audio Booster & Normalizer
- Integrated Web Audio API `GainNode` and `DynamicsCompressorNode` to boost low-volume stream sources up to **200%**.
- Automated compression prevents audio distortion and speaker clipping.

### 16. ⏺️ Live Stream Recorder / Highlight Clip Saver
- One-click **● REC** button (or keyboard shortcut **`R`**) to record live stream segments.
- Live duration counter (`REC 00:24`) and automatic saving to your Downloads folder as `.mp4` / `.webm`.

### 17. 🔢 Digital Cable TV Dialing
- Every channel has a dedicated channel number (e.g. `#01`, `#102`, `#505`).
- Type any numbers on your keyboard to trigger the on-screen display (**`CH 102`**) and tune immediately.

### 18. ⚡ Multi-Server Auto-Healing Failover
- Dual and triple broadcast server feeds for major channels (Server 1 / Server 2 / Server 3).
- Instant fallback automatically switches streams if a network hiccup or geo-block occurs.

### 19. ⏪ 1-Hour DVR Timeshift Catch-Up
- 1-hour backbuffer scrubbing with **-15m**, **-30m**, and **-1h** instant jump buttons.
- One-click **"GO LIVE"** button to snap back to the live broadcast edge instantly.

### 20. 📥 Custom M3U / M3U8 Playlist Importer
- Import your own IPTV playlist via remote URL or local `.m3u` / `.m3u8` file.

## 📥 Installation Guide

### macOS Installation
1. Download **[`LiveTV-v3.7.dmg`](https://github.com/SamAkhtarul/Live-TV/releases/download/v3.7/LiveTV-v3.7.dmg)**.
2. Double-click the `.dmg` file to open the installer disk image.
3. Drag **Live TV.app** into your **Applications** folder.
4. Launch **Live TV** from Launchpad or Applications.
   > **Note for macOS Gatekeeper**: If macOS displays an "unidentified developer" prompt on first launch, open Terminal and run:
   > ```bash
   > xattr -cr "/Applications/Live TV.app"
   > ```
   > Then open Live TV normally.

### Android Installation
1. Download **[`LiveTV-v3.7.apk`](https://github.com/SamAkhtarul/Live-TV/releases/download/v3.7/LiveTV-v3.7.apk)** directly on your Android phone, tablet, or Android TV.
2. Open the downloaded `.apk` file and tap **Install**.
   *(Enable "Allow from this source" in Android Settings if prompted).*
3. Open **Live TV** and start watching.
